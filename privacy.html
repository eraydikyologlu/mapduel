Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

MapDuel Uploader is a personal tool. Only the owner of the MapDuel YouTube channel uses it.

What data we access

The app requests one Google permission: youtube.upload. It uses this permission only to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What we store

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computers. The app does not collect, sell or share any personal data with third parties.

Google API Services

MapDuel Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads also follow the YouTube Terms of Service.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
